# Demo script

Total target: 170 seconds (limit 180). Run it from empty browser storage, with no network and no API key.

## Before the demo

1. Open the app once while online, so the exam and board content are cached. Wait for the offline notice or reload once.
2. Turn off the network (airplane mode or DevTools offline).
3. Clear site data so storage is empty. Keep the service worker, or reopen the cached app.
4. The demo short timer is in Settings. Turn it on after onboarding (step 2 below) so the Paper lasts 60 seconds. The questions and marking do not change.

## Steps

| # | Time | Step | What to show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00 to 0:25 (25 s) | Onboarding | Enter a fictional name, pick Form 5, keep Matematik, choose a language. Switch BM and EN once. Tap "Start with Rakan". |
| 2 | 0:25 to 0:40 (15 s) | Enable demo timer | Open Settings, switch on "Demo short timer for the exam", go back to Rakan. |
| 3 | 0:40 to 1:00 (20 s) | Rakan quiz answer | Tap "Quiz me". Answer one question. Show the feedback and short explanation. |
| 4 | 1:00 to 1:25 (25 s) | Paper with 60 second timer | Open Exam, read the instructions (60 seconds), start. Answer a few questions and show the countdown. |
| 5 | 1:25 to 1:35 (10 s) | Submit or time out | Submit, or let the timer reach zero. |
| 6 | 1:35 to 1:55 (20 s) | Results | Show marks per point, the skema, explanations, the Matematik grade and weak topics. |
| 7 | 1:55 to 2:10 (15 s) | Updated Rakan nudge | Go to Rakan. The nudge now names a weak topic from the result. |
| 8 | 2:10 to 2:40 (30 s) | Pathway board | Open Pathways. Tap "Use my latest exam grade for Matematik". Show the hints, the "may be open" wording and a what-if change. |
| 9 | 2:40 to 2:50 (10 s) | Disclaimer | Point to the "Guidance only" disclaimer on the board. |

Total: 170 seconds.

## Notes

- All names, grades and answers are fictional.
- Say out loud that the board gives guidance only, not official results.
- If a step runs long, skip the language switch in step 1 first.
